The team sent through the wormhole consists of highly trained individuals:

## Potential Habitable Worlds

The Lazarus missions initially identified three potential planets orbiting the black hole Gargantua:

1. Miller's Planet (An ocean world with massive tidal waves)
2. Mann's Planet (An icy, desolate world)
3. Edmunds' Planet (A rocky, desert-like world)

## Mission Status

| Planet | Explorer | Environment | Viability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
